## How the Program Works

Novilla offers two distinct partner tracks so you can choose the model that fits your business. Affiliates promote Novilla products online and earn a commission on every sale they drive. Wholesale partners purchase Novilla products directly at discounted rates to resell through their own storefronts or hospitality operations. Both tracks come with dedicated support and access to Novilla's certified product lineup.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Your Track">
    Decide whether the Affiliate Program or the Wholesale/B2B Program better fits your business model. Affiliates earn commissions on referred sales; wholesale partners buy inventory at volume discounts.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register Your Account">
    Sign up through the appropriate portal — Impact for affiliates or the Novilla Business Portal for wholesale. The process takes less than five minutes and only requires basic business information.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get Approved">
    Affiliate applications are typically reviewed within one business day. Wholesale applications may take slightly longer depending on order volume and business type. You'll receive a confirmation email once your account is active.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start Earning">
    Affiliates receive unique tracking links and promotional assets immediately after approval. Wholesale partners gain access to volume pricing tiers and can place their first order right away.
  </Step>
</Steps>
