If your question isn't covered here, reach out to [affiliates@novilla.us](mailto:affiliates@novilla.us) and the team will be happy to help.

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="When do I get paid?">
  Novilla processes affiliate payouts **on or before the 15th of each month** for commissions that have cleared the validation period.

  Here's the full timeline:
  1. You refer a sale in a given month (e.g., January).
  2. The validation period begins on **the 1st of the following month** (February 1) and lasts **45 days**.
  3. Once validated, your commission is paid out **on or before the 15th** of the next month (April 15 in this example).

  So from sale to payment, you're typically looking at approximately **2.5–3 months total** — a natural result of the validation period allowing time for any returns to be processed before funds are released.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens if a customer returns the product?">
  If a customer returns a product within the eligible return window, the commission associated with that order will be **reversed** from your account. This applies regardless of whether the commission is still pending or has already been approved.

  Novilla offers a **100-night sleep trial** on novillashop.com and a **30-night trial** on novilla.net, so it's worth setting honest expectations with your audience about the return policy. Customers who buy with confidence and understand the product are far less likely to return.

  Reversed commissions will appear in your dashboard. If you believe a reversal was applied in error, contact [affiliates@novilla.us](mailto:affiliates@novilla.us).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I request a free product sample?">
  Yes! Novilla offers **free product samples** for affiliates and content creators who want to review Novilla products firsthand. Authentic reviews from people who've actually used the products tend to perform significantly better than purely promotional content.

  To request a sample, email [pr@novilla.us](mailto:pr@novilla.us) with a brief description of your platform, your audience size, and the type of content you plan to create. The PR team will follow up with availability and next steps.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Does it cost anything to join the Novilla affiliate program?">
  No — joining the Novilla Partner Program is completely **free**. There are no setup fees, monthly fees, or minimum performance requirements to maintain your affiliate status. You simply sign up, get your unique affiliate link, and start promoting.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do cookies work? What if someone clicks my link but doesn't buy right away?">
  Novilla uses a **180-day cookie window**. This means that when someone clicks your affiliate link, a cookie is stored in their browser for 180 days. If they return to Novilla and complete a purchase any time within that 180-day window, you receive credit for the sale — even if they didn't buy on their first visit.

  This is especially valuable for higher-consideration purchases like mattresses, where customers often research for several weeks before deciding. You don't need to worry about losing a commission because someone took their time.

  <Note>
    If a customer clicks multiple affiliate links before purchasing, the platform typically credits the **most recent affiliate link** clicked. Encourage your audience to bookmark your link or return to your content when they're ready to buy.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if my PayPal isn't receiving payments?">
  There are a few common reasons your PayPal may not be receiving payments:

  - **Incorrect email address** — Verify that the PayPal email in your affiliate dashboard exactly matches the email on your PayPal account (including capitalization).
  - **Unverified PayPal account** — Unverified accounts can have receiving limits. Log in to PayPal and complete the verification steps.
  - **PayPal account limitations** — PayPal occasionally places limitations on accounts for security reasons. Check your PayPal account for any notifications or action items.
  - **Payout hasn't been sent yet** — Confirm you're checking after the 15th of the month and that your commissions have cleared the validation period.

  If none of the above resolves the issue, contact [affiliates@novilla.us](mailto:affiliates@novilla.us) with your affiliate ID and the payment date in question.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I track my earnings and sales?">
  You have two ways to track your performance:

  1. **Email notifications** — You'll receive an automatic email for every qualifying order placed through your link, showing the order details and your commission amount.
  2. **Affiliate dashboard** — Log in to [Refersion](https://novilla.refersion.com/) or [Impact](https://app.impact.com/campaign-campaign-info-v2/Novilla.brand) to see real-time data on clicks, conversions, pending commissions, and payout history.

  Your dashboard is updated in real time, so you can check it at any point to see how your promotions are performing. See the [Tracking Your Affiliate Earnings](/payments/tracking) page for a full guide to reading your dashboard stats.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What commission rates can I earn?">
  Novilla affiliate commissions range from **6% to 10% per sale**, depending on your tier and performance level:

  - **Standard affiliates** start at **6% commission**.
  - **Top-performing affiliates** can earn up to **10% commission** based on their sales volume and engagement.

  Your commission is calculated on the order subtotal (before taxes and shipping). To learn more about how to move into a higher performance tier, email [affiliates@novilla.us](mailto:affiliates@novilla.us).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Is there a minimum payout threshold?">
  For specific information on minimum payout thresholds, log in to your affiliate dashboard on [Refersion](https://novilla.refersion.com/) or [Impact](https://app.impact.com/campaign-campaign-info-v2/Novilla.brand) and check your payment settings. If you have questions about your specific account, reach out to [affiliates@novilla.us](mailto:affiliates@novilla.us) for clarification.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if I was not credited for a sale I referred?">
  If you believe a sale wasn't tracked correctly, gather as much information as possible — such as the approximate purchase date, the product the customer bought, and any order confirmation the customer is willing to share — then contact [affiliates@novilla.us](mailto:affiliates@novilla.us). The team can investigate on the back end and manually attribute the commission if the referral is verified.

  To minimize missed tracking in the future, remind your audience to complete their purchase in the same browser session where they clicked your link, without using ad blockers or private browsing mode.
</Accordion>
