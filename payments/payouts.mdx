As a Novilla affiliate, you earn commissions on every qualifying sale you refer — and getting paid is straightforward. All payouts are processed through PayPal on a monthly basis, so having an active PayPal account connected to your affiliate profile is the single most important step to ensure you receive your earnings on time.

## How Payouts Work

Novilla uses a two-phase process before a commission is paid out: a **validation period** followed by a **payment window**. This gives time for any returns or disputes to be resolved before funds are released.

<Steps>
  <Step title="You Refer a Sale">
    A customer clicks your unique affiliate link, makes a purchase, and the order is tracked through Refersion or Impact. You receive an email notification confirming the commission has been logged.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Validation Period (45 Days)">
    Starting from the first day of the month following the sale, your commission enters a 45-day validation period. During this window, the order is verified and any returns or cancellations are processed. If the customer returns the product, the commission is reversed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Payment Processing">
    Once validated, your commission is scheduled for payment. Payouts are sent **on or before the 15th of the month**, covering all commissions validated in the prior period.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Funds in Your PayPal">
    You receive your payout directly to your connected PayPal account. Processing time after the transfer depends on PayPal but is typically instant to 1 business day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payout Timeline at a Glance

| Event | Timing |
|---|---|
| Sale occurs | Day 0 |
| Validation period begins | 1st of the following month |
| Validation period ends | 45 days after period start |
| Commission paid out | On or before the 15th of the month after validation |

<Note>
  For example, if a customer purchases in **January**, the validation period begins **February 1** and runs through approximately **March 17**. Your commission would then be paid out **on or before April 15**.
</Note>

## Payment Method: PayPal

All affiliate commissions are paid exclusively via **PayPal**. Before your first payout, make sure you have completed the following:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create or Log In to PayPal">
    If you don't already have a PayPal account, visit [paypal.com](https://www.paypal.com) to create one. A free Personal account is sufficient for receiving affiliate payments.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect PayPal to Your Affiliate Profile">
    Log in to your affiliate dashboard on [Refersion](https://novilla.refersion.com/) or [Impact](https://app.impact.com/campaign-campaign-info-v2/Novilla.brand) and navigate to your payment settings. Enter your PayPal email address exactly as it appears in your PayPal account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your Email Address">
    Double-check that the PayPal email in your affiliate profile is correct and that your PayPal account is verified. Unverified accounts may have receiving limits that delay your payout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Keep Your Information Updated">
    If you ever change your PayPal email address, update it in your affiliate dashboard immediately to avoid missed or delayed payments.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  If your PayPal email is incorrect or your account has receiving restrictions, your payout may be delayed or returned. Always verify your payment details before the 15th of the month.
</Warning>

## What Happens If a Customer Returns?

If a customer returns a product within the trial period, the associated commission will be **reversed** from your account. This is why the 45-day validation period exists — it ensures that only confirmed, non-returned orders result in a payout. You can monitor any reversals directly in your affiliate dashboard.
