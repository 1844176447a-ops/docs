You have two ways to stay on top of your earnings: automatic email notifications for every new order, and a full-featured affiliate dashboard where you can dig into clicks, conversions, and commission totals at any time.

## Email Notifications

Every time a customer completes a purchase using your affiliate link, Novilla's tracking platform sends you an email confirmation. This notification arrives shortly after the order is placed and typically includes:

- The order date and a reference number
- The product(s) purchased
- Your commission amount for that order
- The commission status (e.g., pending validation)

<Note>
  Email notifications are sent per order, so if you're having a high-traffic day you may receive multiple emails. Check your spam or promotions folder if you're not seeing them in your inbox.
</Note>

## Reading Your Dashboard Stats

Once you're logged in to your dashboard, here's a breakdown of the key metrics you'll see and what they mean for your affiliate business:

<Accordion title="Clicks">
  **Clicks** represent the total number of times someone has clicked on one of your affiliate links. This is the top of your funnel — the more qualified traffic you send to Novilla, the more opportunities you have to earn commissions.

  - A **high click count with low conversions** may suggest your audience needs more context about the products before clicking through.
  - Track which specific links or placements are generating the most clicks to double down on what's working.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Conversions">
  **Conversions** (sometimes labeled as "orders" or "sales") show how many clicks resulted in an actual purchase. Your **conversion rate** is conversions divided by clicks, expressed as a percentage.

  - Industry-average conversion rates for mattress and bedding products typically range from 1%–4%.
  - If your conversion rate is lower, consider adding product reviews, testimonials, or a clear call-to-action near your links.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Commission Earned">
  **Commission earned** reflects the total value of commissions generated by your referrals. This figure is usually broken into two states:

  - **Pending** — commissions that are within the 45-day validation period and not yet approved for payout.
  - **Approved / Payable** — commissions that have cleared validation and are scheduled or have already been paid.

  Always check both columns so you have an accurate picture of your total earnings pipeline.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Payout History">
  Your **payout history** shows a record of all past payments sent to your PayPal account, including the payment date and the amount. Use this to reconcile your earnings and confirm that each monthly payout matches your expected commissions.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Cookie Window">
  Novilla uses a **180-day cookie window**, meaning if a visitor clicks your link today, you'll receive credit for any purchase they make within the next 180 days. Your dashboard may show attributed sales that occurred days or weeks after the original click — this is normal and expected.
</Accordion>

## Tips for Getting the Most From Your Dashboard

<Tip>
  Set a recurring calendar reminder to review your dashboard weekly. Spotting trends early — like a spike in clicks after a particular post — helps you create more of what converts.
</Tip>

<Tip>
  Use the date-range filter in your dashboard to compare performance across different time periods: week over week, month over month, or campaign by campaign.
</Tip>

<Warning>
  If you notice a commission that was marked "approved" but does not appear in your payout, check that your PayPal email in your profile is correct. Incorrect payment details are the most common reason payouts are delayed.
</Warning>

## Reporting a Tracking Discrepancy

If you believe a sale was not tracked correctly — for example, a customer told you they purchased through your link but no commission appeared in your dashboard — contact the Novilla affiliate team at [affiliates@novilla.us](mailto:affiliates@novilla.us) with the order details. Include the approximate purchase date, the product purchased, and any order confirmation information the customer is willing to share.
