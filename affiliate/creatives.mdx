Creative assets are the ready-made promotional materials Novilla provides to help you present their products professionally and persuasively. Whether you prefer visual banner ads, clean text links, or shareable discount codes, your affiliate dashboard gives you everything you need to start promoting right away — no design skills required.

Novilla provides three main types of creative assets for affiliates:

## Finding Your Assets in the Dashboard

All creative assets are accessible from within your affiliate account on Impact or Refersion.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log In to Your Affiliate Dashboard">
    Sign in at [app.impact.com/campaign-campaign-info-v2/Novilla.brand](https://app.impact.com/campaign-campaign-info-v2/Novilla.brand) or [novilla.refersion.com](https://novilla.refersion.com/), depending on which platform you registered with.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Assets or Creatives Section">
    Look for a section labeled **Creatives**, **Assets**, **Promotions**, or **Media**. This is where all banners, text links, and codes are stored and organized.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse and Select What You Need">
    Filter by asset type (banner, text, coupon) or by product category. Preview banners before downloading to make sure the size and style fit your placement.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the Embed Code or Download the Asset">
    For banners, copy the provided HTML embed code — it already includes your tracking link. For text links, copy the URL. For discount codes, copy the alphanumeric code to share with your audience.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Using Banners Effectively

Novilla banners come in standard sizes suited for website headers, sidebars, and inline article placements. They're pre-designed to highlight key selling points — free shipping, the 100-night sleep trial, and product highlights.

<Tip>
  Place banners contextually within relevant content rather than in generic sidebar slots. A banner promoting the AiryFlow™ mattress embedded within a mattress comparison article will outperform the same banner in an unrelated page's sidebar.
</Tip>

<Accordion title="Best banner placement locations">
  - **Within review articles** — Inline banners placed after the introduction or before the verdict section catch readers who are already engaged.
  - **Comparison pages** — A banner highlighting Novilla's 100-night trial is highly effective on pages where readers are weighing their options.
  - **Dedicated landing pages** — If you build a standalone Novilla promo page, use a hero-style banner near the top paired with a direct affiliate link.
</Accordion>

## Using Text Links

Text links are the most versatile asset type — they embed into any piece of written content and don't depend on image loading or ad blockers. Use them as anchor text in blog posts, product descriptions, email newsletters, and social bios.

<Tip>
  Combine a text link with a brief personal endorsement for the highest conversion rate. For example: *"I've been sleeping on the Novilla Bliss for three months — it's the best purchase I've made this year. [Check the current price here](#)."*
</Tip>

## Sharing Discount Codes

Your audience can use a **5% discount code** at checkout on their Novilla order. Sharing this code in your content gives followers a concrete reason to click through and buy, and it signals that you've negotiated something special for them.

**Where to use discount codes:**
- Mentioned verbally in YouTube videos ("Use my code at checkout for 5% off")
- Pinned in Instagram Stories or as a text overlay
- Highlighted in a callout box in blog posts
- Featured in email newsletter subject lines or preview text

<Note>
  The 5% customer discount is applied before your commission is calculated. This is already factored into the commission structure — you don't need to choose between offering the discount and earning your full commission rate.
</Note>

## Creative Best Practices

<Accordion title="Keep your creatives fresh">
  Rotate banners and update your discount code placements periodically. Audiences tune out assets they've seen repeatedly, and new creative can re-engage readers who didn't convert the first time.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Match the creative to the content">
  Use creatives that are directly relevant to the article or video they appear in. A banner for a memory foam mattress works well in a "best mattresses for back pain" post; a bedding banner fits better in a bedroom décor guide.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Always disclose paid partnerships">
  Whether you're using a banner, text link, or discount code, include a clear affiliate disclosure on that page or in that video. Transparency is both a legal requirement and an audience trust signal.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Test placements and track performance">
  Use the reporting tools in your Impact or Refersion dashboard to see which links and banners are generating the most clicks and conversions. Double down on what's working and replace underperforming placements.
</Accordion>
