Every time a customer completes a purchase through your affiliate link, you earn a percentage of the order value as your commission. Novilla's program is structured as a performance tier model — the more sales you drive, the higher your commission rate climbs, rewarding your growth over time.

## Commission Tiers

Novilla pays between **6% and 10% commission** on each qualifying sale. Your rate is determined by your overall sales performance and account standing. As you hit higher volume thresholds, your account can be upgraded to a higher tier by the affiliate team.

<Info>
  Tier thresholds and upgrade criteria are managed by the Novilla affiliate team. To ask about your current tier or a potential upgrade, contact **affiliates@novilla.us**.
</Info>

| Performance Tier | Commission Rate |
|---|---|
| Standard | 6% |
| Growing | 8% |
| Top Performer | 10% |

## How Commissions Are Calculated

Your commission is calculated as a percentage of the **order subtotal** — the product price after any customer-facing discounts but before taxes and shipping.

### Sample Earning Calculations

|---|---|---|---|
| AiryFlow™ Mattress | $300 | 8% | **$24.00** |
| Bliss Mattress | $500 | 8% | **$40.00** |
| Vitality Hybrid Mattress | $800 | 10% | **$80.00** |

<Tip>
  Mattresses tend to be your highest-earning product category due to their higher average order value. Featuring mattress reviews, comparisons, and sleep guides in your content is a reliable way to maximize earnings per conversion.
</Tip>

## The 5% Customer Discount

Your referred customers can apply a **5% discount** to their order at checkout. This discount is already built into the program — you don't need to create a custom code for it to be available.

<Note>
  The customer discount is applied to the order subtotal before your commission is calculated. For example, if a customer uses the discount on a $500 mattress, your commission is calculated on the discounted price (~$475).
</Note>

## When Commissions Are Confirmed

Commissions move through two stages before you're paid:

1. **Pending** — A commission is recorded as pending immediately after a referred purchase is completed. It stays in this state during the customer's return/trial window.
2. **Confirmed** — Once the order passes the return period without a refund or return, the commission is marked as confirmed and queued for the next payout cycle.

<Info>
  Novilla mattresses come with a **100-night sleep trial**, which means commission confirmation may take several months for mattress orders. Smaller items like bedding and frames typically confirm faster.
</Info>

## Returns and Refunds

If a customer returns their order or receives a full refund, the associated commission is **reversed** and will not be paid out. Partial refunds result in a proportional reduction to the commission amount.

<Warning>
  Commissions on orders that are charged back, disputed, or flagged as fraudulent will be voided. Repeatedly driving low-quality or fraudulent traffic can result in account suspension.
</Warning>

## Payout Schedule

Confirmed commissions from the previous calendar month are paid on or before the **15th of each month** via **PayPal**. Make sure your PayPal address is correctly entered in your affiliate dashboard to avoid payment delays.

<Accordion title="Example Payout Timeline">
  - You refer a sale on **March 3rd**
  - The customer keeps their order; commission confirms in **late April** (after the 100-night trial window)
  - The confirmed commission appears in your **May payout**, paid on or before **May 15th**

  Smaller, non-mattress items often confirm within 30 days, resulting in faster payout cycles.
</Accordion>
