The Novilla Affiliate Program is a straightforward way to earn money by recommending sleep products you believe in. Once you're approved, you receive a unique tracking link, share it with your audience, and earn a commission every time someone completes a purchase through that link — all with zero inventory or customer service on your end.

## The Affiliate Loop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign Up and Get Approved">
    Apply through either of Novilla's affiliate platforms — **Impact** or **Refersion**. Approval is typically quick, and you'll receive a confirmation email once your account is active.

    - **Impact:** [app.impact.com/campaign-campaign-info-v2/Novilla.brand](https://app.impact.com/campaign-campaign-info-v2/Novilla.brand)
    - **Refersion:** [novilla.refersion.com](https://novilla.refersion.com/)

    <Tip>
      If you have questions during sign-up or want to discuss a partnership before applying, reach out to the affiliate team at **affiliates@novilla.us**.
    </Tip>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Grab Your Unique Tracking Links">
    After logging in to your affiliate dashboard, you'll find your personal tracking links for any Novilla product page — mattresses, bed frames, bedding, sofas, and more. Every link contains a unique identifier tied to your account so every click and sale is credited back to you.

    Your dashboard also gives you access to:
    - Ready-made banners and text link creatives
    - Discount codes to share with your audience (5% off for their order)
    - Performance reports showing clicks, conversions, and pending commissions
  </Step>

  <Step title="Promote to Your Audience">
    Share your links anywhere your audience engages with your content — blog posts, YouTube videos, social media, email newsletters, or product review sites. Novilla supports **text links**, **banners**, and **deals/coupon** promotions.

    <Note>
      Some promotion methods are not permitted. POP/popunder traffic and bidding on Novilla's trademarked terms in paid search are both prohibited. See the [Tracking Links](/affiliate/tracking-links) page for the full policy.
    </Note>
  </Step>

  <Step title="Customer Clicks, Shops, and Buys">
    When someone clicks your link, a **180-day tracking cookie** is set in their browser. That means you get credit for a purchase even if they don't buy right away — as long as they return and complete their order within 180 days of that first click.

    Your audience also benefits: they can apply a **5% discount** on their order, making the recommendation a win for them too.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Earn Your Commission">
    Once a referred order is confirmed (past the return window), your commission is locked in. Novilla pays **6%–10% commission** on each sale, with higher rates available as your performance grows.

    Payouts are processed **monthly via PayPal**, on or before the **15th of each month** for the previous month's confirmed commissions.

    <Tip>
      Affiliates are also eligible for up to **60% off Novilla mattresses** — perfect if you want to try the products firsthand before recommending them, or for personal use.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You Can Promote

Novilla's full product catalog is available to affiliates, including:

- **Memory Foam Mattresses** — Bliss, AiryFlow™
- **Hybrid Mattresses** — Vitality, Grove
- **Bed Frames, Bedding, and Sofas**

Every mattress order includes **free U.S. shipping**, a **100-night sleep trial**, and a **10-year warranty** — making them an easy, low-risk recommendation for your audience.
