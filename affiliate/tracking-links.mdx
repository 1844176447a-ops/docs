Your affiliate tracking link is the core tool that connects your promotions to your commissions. Every click that passes through your link is recorded in your dashboard, and every resulting purchase is attributed to your account — even if the customer doesn't buy until months later. This page walks you through how to get your links, use them effectively, and stay within program guidelines.

## Getting Your Links

Your tracking links are generated inside your affiliate dashboard on either the **Impact** or **Refersion** platform — whichever you used to sign up.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log In to Your Dashboard">
    Sign in to your affiliate account:
    - **Impact:** [app.impact.com/campaign-campaign-info-v2/Novilla.brand](https://app.impact.com/campaign-campaign-info-v2/Novilla.brand)
    - **Refersion:** [novilla.refersion.com](https://novilla.refersion.com/)
  </Step>

  <Step title="Navigate to Links or Promotions">
    Inside your dashboard, look for the **Links**, **Promotions**, or **Assets** section. Here you'll find a default homepage link as well as the ability to generate deep links to specific product pages.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Copy or Generate Your Link">
    Copy the pre-generated link for the product or page you want to promote, or use the deep-link generator (see below) to build a link pointing directly to a specific mattress, bed frame, or other product page. Your unique affiliate ID is embedded automatically.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The 180-Day Cookie Window

When a visitor clicks your affiliate link, a tracking cookie is placed in their browser. This cookie persists for **180 days** — one of the longest windows in the sleep products industry — meaning you earn credit for a purchase as long as the customer returns and completes their order within six months of clicking.

<Info>
  The 180-day cookie is especially valuable for mattress shoppers, who often research and compare products for weeks or months before buying. Your early referrals can still convert long after your content goes live.
</Info>

<Accordion title="How Cookie Attribution Works">
  If a customer clicks your link on Day 1 but doesn't buy until Day 90, the sale is still attributed to you. However, if the same customer later clicks a different affiliate's link before purchasing, attribution typically goes to the **most recent click** (last-click attribution model). The 180-day window gives you a strong opportunity to be the last touchpoint before a purchase.
</Accordion>

## Deep Linking to Product Pages

Instead of always linking to the Novilla homepage, deep linking lets you send visitors directly to the product page most relevant to your content. This reduces friction and improves your conversion rate.

**How to create a deep link:**
1. In your dashboard, find the deep-link or custom link generator tool.
2. Enter the URL of the specific Novilla product page you want to link to (e.g., the AiryFlow™ mattress page).
3. The tool wraps that URL with your affiliate tracking parameters.
4. Copy the resulting link and use it in your content.

<Tip>
  Deep links consistently outperform homepage links because they land visitors exactly where they're ready to buy. If you write a review of the GreyZen mattress, link directly to that product page — not the homepage.
</Tip>

## Embedding Links in Your Content

Your tracking links work anywhere clickable text or URLs are supported. Here are best practices for each major channel:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Blog and Website" icon="browser">
    Embed links as natural anchor text within your article copy (e.g., "the [Novilla AiryFlow™ mattress](#)"). Also add a dedicated "Shop Now" or "Check Price" call-to-action button near the top and bottom of reviews.
  </Card>
  <Card title="YouTube" icon="circle-play">
    Add your affiliate link in the video description, ideally as the first or second link. Mention the link verbally in the video and pin a comment with the link for extra visibility.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Social Media" icon="share-nodes">
    On platforms that support clickable links (Instagram bio, TikTok bio, Pinterest, Facebook posts), use a link-in-bio tool or post the link directly. For Stories and short-form video, direct followers to your bio link.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Email Newsletter" icon="envelope">
    Embed your link behind a clearly labeled call-to-action button or hyperlinked text. Disclose the affiliate relationship in your email per FTC guidelines and your email platform's terms.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What Is and Is Not Allowed

Novilla's program has clear guidelines about how you can and cannot promote your links. Violating these rules can result in commission reversals or account termination.

### Allowed Promotion Methods

- **Text links** embedded in editorial content, reviews, and blog posts
- **Banner ads** placed on your website or in newsletters
- **Deals and coupon promotions** featuring the customer discount code

### Not Allowed

<Warning>
  The following promotion methods are strictly prohibited and will result in commission voidance and potential account suspension:

  - **POP/popunder traffic** — Do not use pop-up or popunder ad networks to drive traffic to Novilla links.
  - **Trademark bidding** — Do not bid on "Novilla," "Novilla mattress," or any other Novilla brand terms in paid search campaigns (Google Ads, Bing Ads, etc.).
</Warning>

If you're unsure whether a specific promotion method is allowed, email **affiliates@novilla.us** before proceeding.

## Tips for Maximizing Click-Through

<Accordion title="Use contextual anchor text">
  Generic text like "click here" performs poorly. Instead, use descriptive anchor text like "Novilla Bliss memory foam mattress" or "Novilla's 100-night sleep trial" — phrases that match what a reader is already thinking about.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Place links above the fold">
  Don't wait until the end of a long article to include your link. Add a prominent link or "Check Price" button near the top of your content so readers who are already ready to buy don't have to scroll to find it.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Disclose your affiliate relationship">
  Always include a clear affiliate disclosure near the top of any page or piece of content containing your links. This is both an FTC requirement and a trust-building practice that your audience will respect.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Link to the most relevant page">
  Match your link destination to your content. A review of the Vitality Hybrid mattress should link to that specific product page, not the homepage. Relevant landing pages convert significantly better.
</Accordion>
