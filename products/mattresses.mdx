## The Novilla Mattress Lineup

    ### Bliss Memory Foam Mattress

    **Starting Price:** From **$119.99**

    **Key Features:**
    - Gel memory foam comfort layer for pressure relief

    - CertiPUR-US certified materials throughout

    **Certifications:** CertiPUR-US®

    **Affiliate Tip:** The Bliss is your highest-converting mattress for general audiences. Its price point, review count, and Amazon presence make it an easy recommendation for budget-conscious buyers. Lead with "most popular" and "30,000+ reviews" — social proof closes this one.

    ### AiryFlow™ Cooling Foam Mattress

    **Starting Price:** From **$199.99**

    Launched in **February 2026**, the AiryFlow™ is Novilla's most advanced foam mattress, built specifically for sleepers who overheat at night. It features a proprietary 360° AiryFlow™ Cooling System that moves heat away from the body from every angle.

    **Key Features:**
    - **360° AiryFlow™ Cooling System** — Proprietary multi-directional cooling architecture
    - **NanoGEL™ Memory Foam** — Advanced nano-particle gel infusion for superior heat dissipation
    - **Ice Silk Cover** — Cool-to-the-touch fabric surface for an instant cooling sensation
    - **3D Breathable Mesh Sides** — Full-perimeter airflow, not just top-surface cooling
    - **Green Tea Base Layer** — Freshness and odor control at the foundation

    **Best For:** Hot sleepers, couples with different temperature preferences, and anyone in warmer climates.

    **Certifications:** CertiPUR-US®

    **Affiliate Tip:** The AiryFlow™ is your premium foam offering and newest launch — ideal for content focused on sleep quality and temperature. Use it in "best mattress for hot sleepers" content. The $199.99 entry price is still highly competitive for a cooling-focused mattress at this feature level.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="Vitality Hybrid 12-inch">
    ### Vitality Hybrid Mattress (12")

    **Starting Price:** From **$328.99**

    The Vitality Hybrid is Novilla's award-winning flagship model, combining individually wrapped pocket springs with multiple comfort foam layers for a medium-firm feel with zoned support. It earned the **Sleepopolis Best Firm Mattress** award.

    **Key Features:**
    - **Individually Wrapped Pocket Springs** — Respond independently to reduce motion transfer
    - **Gel Foam Comfort Layer** — Contouring with temperature regulation
    - **Airflow Foam Layer** — Dedicated breathability layer between comfort and support zones
    - **Bamboo Cover** — Naturally breathable and sustainably sourced
    - **Zoned Support System** — Firmer under hips and lumbar, softer under shoulders
    - **Medium-Firm Feel** — Rated **5–6 out of 10** on the firmness scale

    **Best For:** Back and stomach sleepers, combination sleepers, those seeking spinal alignment and edge support.

    **Certifications:** CertiPUR-US®

    **Affiliate Tip:** Lead with the Sleepopolis award and the zoned support story. This converts well with audiences seeking a "real mattress" feel — buyers who want the responsiveness of springs with the comfort of foam. The $328.99 entry price is exceptional for a true hybrid at this quality level.

## HSA/FSA Eligibility

All Novilla mattresses are eligible for purchase using **Health Savings Account (HSA)** or **Flexible Spending Account (FSA)** funds. This is a valuable detail for audiences managing back pain, sleep disorders, or other qualifying health conditions — it effectively makes the mattress more affordable with pre-tax dollars.

<Tip>
  Include HSA/FSA eligibility in your content targeting health-focused audiences or buyers aged 35+. Many buyers don't realize they can use these funds for a mattress purchase — you'll stand out by surfacing this benefit.
</Tip>
