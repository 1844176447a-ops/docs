## CertiPUR-US®

CertiPUR-US is the most widely recognized foam certification in the United States. Foam certified under this program has been tested by independent, accredited laboratories and verified to meet strict content, emissions, and durability standards.

**What CertiPUR-US guarantees — Novilla foam is made WITHOUT:**

<Accordion title="Ozone Depleters">
  CertiPUR-US certified foam is manufactured without ozone-depleting chemicals, which are harmful to both the environment and human health. Novilla's foam production meets these standards across all product lines.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Mercury, Lead, and Heavy Metals">
  All Novilla foam is tested and confirmed to be free from mercury, lead, and other heavy metals. This is particularly important for households with children or individuals with sensitivities.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Formaldehyde">
  No formaldehyde is present in Novilla's certified foam layers. Formaldehyde is a common concern with lower-quality foam products and a frequent source of off-gassing complaints.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="PBDE Flame Retardants">
  Polybrominated diphenyl ethers (PBDEs) are banned under CertiPUR-US. Novilla uses safer alternative flame retardant technologies that meet federal flammability standards without the health risks associated with PBDEs.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Phthalates (regulated by the Consumer Product Safety Commission)">
  Novilla's foam is free from phthalates at levels restricted by the CPSC — chemicals that can disrupt hormones and are of particular concern for children and pregnant individuals.
</Accordion>

**CertiPUR-US also requires:**
- Low VOC (Volatile Organic Compound) emissions for indoor air quality — less than 0.5 parts per million
- Durability testing to ensure foam performs as claimed over the long term

<Tip>
  When a customer asks "Is this mattress safe?" — CertiPUR-US is your answer. Mention that Novilla's foam has been independently tested and certified by laboratories accredited under the CertiPUR-US program. This resolves the concern immediately and builds trust.
</Tip>

## OEKO-TEX® Standard 100

OEKO-TEX Standard 100 is one of the world's best-known labels for textiles tested for harmful substances. Unlike certifications that only test the finished product, OEKO-TEX tests **every component** — fibers, threads, buttons, zippers, and dyes — at every stage of production.

**What OEKO-TEX certification means for Novilla customers:**

- All fabric covers (mattress covers, bedding, pillow covers) are free from harmful substances
- Testing covers over **100 substances** that are harmful or potentially harmful to human health
- Applies to products intended for direct skin contact — ensuring the materials your customers sleep on and in are rigorously vetted
- Independent certification renewed annually to maintain standards

**OEKO-TEX classes relevant to sleep products:**

| Class | Application | Relevance |
|-------|-------------|-----------|
| Class I | Baby products (direct skin contact) | Highest standard |
| Class II | Direct skin contact products | Mattress covers, bedding |
| Class III | Not in direct contact with skin | Internal layers |

<Note>
  OEKO-TEX certification is especially compelling for parents buying mattresses for children, individuals with chemical sensitivities, and eco-conscious consumers. It's a strong secondary selling point after CertiPUR-US.
</Note>

## ISPA Membership

The **International Sleep Products Association (ISPA)** is the leading trade organization representing mattress manufacturers and suppliers. ISPA membership reflects Novilla's standing as a recognized, professional participant in the sleep products industry — not a fly-by-night brand.

**What ISPA membership means:**
- Adherence to industry standards and best practices
- Participation in advocacy for consumer safety in the sleep products sector
- Access to industry research and standards that inform product development
- Commitment to responsible manufacturing and business practices
