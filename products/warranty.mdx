Every Novilla mattress comes with a **10-year limited warranty** — one of the strongest guarantees in the direct-to-consumer mattress category. This warranty covers structural and manufacturing defects for a full decade, giving customers confidence that their investment is protected long after the 100-night sleep trial has ended. For you as a partner, the warranty is a key trust signal: it tells customers that Novilla stands behind the quality of their products for the long haul, not just through the return window.

## What the Warranty Covers

The 10-year limited warranty covers defects in materials and workmanship that affect the structural integrity or performance of the mattress. Specifically, this includes:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Structural Defects" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Any defect in the mattress structure — including the foam layers, spring system (in hybrid models), and foundational components — that compromises the mattress's intended function and support.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Manufacturing Defects" icon="gear">
    Defects that arise from the production process, including issues with stitching, cover integrity, foam consistency, and assembly that are not caused by use or external factors.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Sagging Beyond Normal Wear" icon="chart-line">
    Visible indentation or sagging in the mattress surface that exceeds normal wear and is not the result of improper use, inadequate support, or physical damage.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Coil and Spring Defects (Hybrid Models)" icon="circle-nodes">
    For the Vitality Hybrid and Grove Hybrid, the warranty covers defects in the individually wrapped pocket spring system, including broken or deformed coils that affect support.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## What the Warranty Does Not Cover

The 10-year warranty is a limited warranty — it covers defects, not wear and tear or misuse. The following are typically not covered:

<Accordion title="Normal softening and comfort changes">
  All foam mattresses naturally soften and conform over time with regular use. This is not a defect — it's a characteristic of memory foam and does not indicate a material or structural failure. The warranty does not cover the gradual change in feel that comes with years of use.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Physical damage from misuse">
  Damage caused by improper handling, accidents, stains, burns, cuts, or abuse is not covered. This includes damage that occurs during transport after delivery.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Inadequate foundation or support">
  If a mattress is used on a foundation or bed frame that does not provide adequate support (e.g., broken slats, sagging platform, incompatible base), resulting indentation or damage is not covered under the warranty.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Comfort preferences">
  If a customer decides the mattress is too firm, too soft, or simply not to their preference, this is a comfort issue — not a defect. Comfort preferences are addressed by the 100-night sleep trial, not the warranty.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Commercial or non-residential use">
  The warranty applies to residential use only. Mattresses used in hotels, rental properties, dormitories, or other commercial settings may not be covered.
</Accordion>

## How to File a Warranty Claim

If a customer believes their mattress has a defect covered under the 10-year warranty, here is the process:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Identify and Document the Defect">
    Take clear photographs or video of the defect. For sagging, use a straight edge (ruler or yardstick) across the mattress surface to show the depth of indentation. Capture the mattress label with the model name and serial number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Gather Proof of Purchase">
    Locate the original order confirmation email, receipt, or any documentation showing the purchase date and product details. This is required to validate the warranty.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact Novilla Support">
    Submit the claim through the Novilla contact page at [novillashop.com/pages/contact-us](https://www.novillashop.com/pages/contact-us). Include:
    - Photos/video of the defect
    - Proof of purchase
    - Description of the issue and when it appeared
  </Step>
  <Step title="Warranty Review">
    Novilla's support team will review the claim and documentation. They may request additional information or photos if needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Resolution">
    If the claim is approved, Novilla will offer a repair, replacement, or other resolution at their discretion, consistent with the warranty terms.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  As a partner, you don't need to handle warranty claims directly. When a customer asks about warranties, point them to [novillashop.com/pages/contact-us](https://www.novillashop.com/pages/contact-us) and reassure them that all Novilla mattresses are backed by a full 10-year limited warranty. This answer handles the objection completely.
</Tip>

## Warranty vs. Sleep Trial: Understanding the Difference

Customers sometimes confuse the sleep trial and the warranty. Here's a clear breakdown:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="100-Night Sleep Trial">
    **Purpose:** Allows customers to try the mattress and return it if it's not the right fit — for **any reason**.

    **Duration:** 100 nights from delivery (novillashop.com) or 30 nights (novilla.net)

    **Outcome:** Full refund, free return in the U.S.

    **Customer question it answers:** "What if I don't like it?"
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="10-Year Limited Warranty">
    **Purpose:** Protects customers against **manufacturing and structural defects** that develop over the life of the mattress.

    **Duration:** 10 years from purchase date

    **Outcome:** Repair or replacement at Novilla's discretion

    **Customer question it answers:** "What if it breaks down or fails?"
  </Tab>
</Tabs>
