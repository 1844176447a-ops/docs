Beyond the mattress and bed frame, Novilla offers a full range of bedding accessories and home comfort products that let you build a complete sleep story for your audience. Made from eco-friendly, organic materials, these products extend the Novilla brand promise — quality sleep, sustainably made — into every layer of the bed. For partners, bedding accessories represent a high-value upsell opportunity: customers who've already decided to trust Novilla for their mattress are primed to complete the setup with matching accessories.

    Protecting a new mattress is one of the first things Novilla recommends to customers — and for good reason. A mattress protector preserves the mattress condition, which is important for maintaining warranty coverage.

    **Key Features:**
    - Waterproof protection layer
    - Breathable materials that don't trap heat
    - Fitted design for secure placement

    **Affiliate Tip:** Mattress protectors are among the easiest upsells in the category. Position them as "protecting your investment" — a $30–50 protector extends the life of a $300+ mattress.     **Affiliate Tip:** Toppers are excellent for audiences who like their current mattress but want to adjust the feel, or who are buying a firm mattress and want a softer surface. "Make your firm mattress softer" content targeting Vitality Hybrid buyers is a natural fit.

## Why Bedding Accessories Convert Well

<Accordion title="Lower price point = easier first purchase">
  A $35 pillow or mattress protector is a lower-stakes purchase than a $300 mattress. Some customers convert on accessories first, then return for a mattress. Building content around accessories can seed long-term Novilla conversions.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Repeat purchase potential">
  Unlike mattresses (typically purchased once every 7–10 years), pillows, comforters, and protectors are replaced more frequently. Audiences who trust your recommendation for a Novilla mattress are likely to return for bedding refreshes.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Eco-friendly angle for sustainability audiences">
  Novilla's organic, eco-certified bedding materials resonate strongly with sustainability-focused audiences. If you create content around sustainable living, slow home, or eco-conscious consumption, Novilla bedding is a natural fit.
</Accordion>
