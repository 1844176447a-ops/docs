## Certifications at a Glance

All Novilla mattresses meet rigorous third-party safety and quality standards:

- **CertiPUR-US®** — Foam certified free from ozone depleters, heavy metals, formaldehyde, and harmful chemicals
- **OEKO-TEX®** — Textiles tested for harmful substances at every stage of production
- **ISPA Member** — International Sleep Products Association membership
- **HSA/FSA Eligible** — Mattresses can be purchased with pre-tax health savings dollars

For a full breakdown of what each certification means, see the [Certifications page](/products/certifications).
