Registering for the Novilla Partner Program takes less than five minutes regardless of which track you choose. The Affiliate Program uses the Impact platform, a trusted affiliate network with a clean dashboard for tracking clicks, conversions, and payouts. The Wholesale Program uses the Novilla Business Portal, where you manage orders, invoices, and volume pricing directly. Follow the steps below for the path that fits your business.

---

## Register as an Affiliate

The affiliate application is handled entirely through Impact, Novilla's affiliate network partner. Before you begin, gather the information listed below so the form goes quickly.

### What You'll Need

- Your full legal name or business name
- A working email address (this becomes your Impact login)
- Your website URL, social media profile, or primary promotion channel
- A brief description of how you plan to promote Novilla products
- Your PayPal email address for monthly payouts
- Your tax information (W-9 for U.S. residents, or equivalent for international applicants)

<Note>
  You do not need a minimum follower count or monthly traffic threshold to apply. Novilla reviews all applications individually and considers audience relevance and content quality alongside audience size.
</Note>

### Affiliate Sign-Up Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the Affiliate Sign-Up Page">
    Navigate to [Novilla's affiliate campaign page on Impact](https://app.impact.com/campaign-campaign-info-v2/Novilla.brand). If you already have an Impact account, log in. If not, click **Create Account** to register a new Impact publisher profile.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create or Log In to Your Impact Account">
    Fill in your name, email address, and password to create a new Impact publisher account. If you already use Impact for other programs, log in with your existing credentials — you can apply to Novilla's program from within your existing dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Your Publisher Profile">
    Add your website or primary promotional channel URL. Select the content categories that best describe your platform (e.g., Home & Garden, Lifestyle, Health & Wellness). Write a short summary of your audience and how you intend to promote Novilla products — this is the most important field for a fast approval.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Payout Details">
    Add your PayPal email address under the payment settings. Novilla issues monthly payouts to this address once you meet the minimum payout threshold. U.S.-based affiliates must also submit a W-9 form before their first payout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Your Application">
    Review your profile details and click **Apply to Program**. You'll receive an automated confirmation email from Impact immediately. Novilla's team typically reviews applications within **one business day**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Write your promotional channel description in specific terms — "I run a mattress review blog with 20,000 monthly readers focused on back pain relief" converts far better than "I have a website about home products." The more context you give, the faster your approval.
</Tip>

## Register for Wholesale / B2B

Wholesale registration is handled through the Novilla Business Portal. You'll need to provide basic business credentials to verify that you're a legitimate reseller or commercial buyer.

### What You'll Need

- Business legal name and trading name (if different)
- Business address and contact phone number
- Business email address
- Business type (e.g., retail store, e-commerce, hospitality, interior design)
- Resale certificate or business license number (where applicable)
- Estimated monthly or annual purchase volume
- Your EIN or VAT number (for tax documentation)

<Note>
  Novilla may request supporting documents such as a copy of your resale certificate or business registration to verify your wholesale eligibility. Having these ready speeds up the review process.
</Note>

### Wholesale Sign-Up Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the B2B Registration Page">
    Open the [Novilla B2B Registration Form](https://business.sweetnight.com/pages/b2b-registration) on the Novilla Business Portal. This is the dedicated entry point for all wholesale and commercial partnership inquiries.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Business Information">
    Fill in your business name, address, contact details, and business type. Select the product categories you're most interested in carrying — mattresses, bed frames, bedding, sofas, or the full catalog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Provide Your Resale Credentials">
    Enter your resale certificate number or business license information. If you operate in a state or jurisdiction that does not issue resale certificates, note that in the relevant field and Novilla's team will follow up.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe Your Purchase Volume">
    Indicate your expected monthly or annual unit volume. This helps Novilla's wholesale team assign you the correct pricing tier from the start. Remember: orders of 2–11 units receive 10% off, and orders of 20+ units receive up to 30% off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and Await Confirmation">
    Submit the form. Novilla's wholesale team will review your application and follow up by email, typically within two to three business days. Large-volume accounts may receive a personal call from the wholesale team to discuss custom pricing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  If you need pricing information before committing to the registration process, email [affiliates@novilla.us](mailto:affiliates@novilla.us) with your estimated order volume and business type. The team can provide a quote and guide you through registration.
</Tip>
