Once you've submitted your application, the hard part is over. Novilla's team reviews every application personally to ensure a high-quality partner network — this means approvals are deliberate, but they're also fast. Most affiliate applications receive a decision within one business day. Use the time between submission and approval to familiarize yourself with Novilla's product catalog and plan your first piece of promotional content or your initial order.

---

## Affiliate Approval

### Timeline

Affiliate applications submitted through Impact are typically reviewed and decided within **one business day**. You'll receive an email directly from Impact notifying you of the outcome. If your application is approved, the email contains a direct link to your affiliate dashboard. If more information is needed, Novilla's team will reach out to the email address on your Impact account.

<Note>
  Applications submitted on weekends or U.S. public holidays may take an additional business day. If you haven't heard back after three business days, check your spam folder first, then email [affiliates@novilla.us](mailto:affiliates@novilla.us) with your Impact account email to follow up.
</Note>

### What Happens After Approval

Once your application is approved, Impact sends you a welcome email with everything you need to start earning. Here's what becomes available immediately:

- **Affiliate dashboard** — Track clicks, conversions, commission earned, and payout history in real time
- **Tracking links** — Generate deep links to any page on the Novilla website; each link is tagged with your unique publisher ID
- **Promotional assets** — Access banners, product images, and copy-ready promotional text in multiple sizes and formats
- **Your customer discount code** — A unique 5% discount code you can share with your audience to drive conversions
- **Product sample eligibility** — Instructions for requesting free product samples for review purposes

### Accessing Your Dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log In to Impact">
    Go to [app.impact.com](https://app.impact.com) and sign in with the email and password you used during registration.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Your Campaigns">
    From the Impact home screen, click **My Campaigns** in the left sidebar. Select **Novilla** from your list of active programs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate Your First Tracking Link">
    Click **Links & Assets** within the Novilla campaign. Use the **Link Builder** tool to create a tracking link to any product page, the homepage, or a sale landing page. Copy the generated URL — this is the link you'll embed in your content.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download Promotional Assets">
    Still within **Links & Assets**, browse the creative library for banners and product images. Download the sizes that fit your platform — blog sidebar, email header, Instagram story, etc.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Tips for Faster Approval

<Accordion title="Complete your Impact profile before applying">
  A complete publisher profile — including a verified website or social channel, a filled-in bio, and accurate traffic category tags — signals professionalism to Novilla's review team and shortens the approval cycle significantly.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Write a specific promotional description">
  The "How will you promote this program?" field carries the most weight in the review. Describe your audience, your content format, and your specific plan for featuring Novilla products. Vague answers like "I will post on social media" are the most common cause of delayed approvals.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Ensure your channel is publicly accessible">
  Novilla's team will visit your website or social profile during review. Make sure your content is live and publicly visible — password-protected or empty channels may result in a hold on your application.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Apply with a professional email address">
  Using a domain-matched email (e.g., yourname@yourblog.com) rather than a generic Gmail or Hotmail address reinforces the legitimacy of your application and often results in faster decisions.
</Accordion>

## Wholesale Approval

Wholesale applications are reviewed by Novilla's B2B team and typically receive a response within **two to three business days**. Accounts with high projected order volumes may receive a direct call from the wholesale team to confirm details and discuss custom pricing before access is granted.

### What Happens After Wholesale Approval

Once your wholesale account is approved, you receive an email with login credentials for the [Novilla Business Portal](https://business.sweetnight.com/). Inside the portal, you can:

- Browse the full product catalog with wholesale pricing applied to your account tier
- Place orders directly and track fulfillment status
- Access product specification sheets, certifications, and high-resolution imagery for your storefront
- Review your volume discount tier and see how additional units affect your per-unit cost

<Info>
  Your initial discount tier is assigned based on the purchase volume you indicated during registration. If your actual order volume grows, contact [affiliates@novilla.us](mailto:affiliates@novilla.us) to have your tier reviewed and upgraded.
</Info>
