Novilla's Partner Program is built around two distinct tracks, each designed for a different type of business relationship. The **Affiliate Program** rewards you for driving traffic and sales to Novilla's website — you promote, customers buy, and you earn a commission. The **Wholesale/B2B Program** lets you purchase Novilla products directly at discounted rates so you can resell them through your own channels. Both tracks give you access to a catalog of certified, award-winning sleep products that your customers already trust.

## Affiliate Program

The Affiliate Program is ideal if you have an established online audience — a blog, YouTube channel, Instagram following, TikTok account, or review website — and want to earn passive income by recommending products you believe in. You never handle inventory; Novilla fulfills every order and you collect your commission.

### Who Should Apply for the Affiliate Program?

The Affiliate Program is a strong fit if you:

- Publish content about home, sleep, health, or lifestyle topics
- Have an active social media presence or email newsletter
- Run a comparison or review website in the mattress or furniture niche
- Want to monetize existing traffic without managing inventory or fulfillment

## Wholesale / B2B Program

The Wholesale Program is designed for retailers, interior designers, property managers, hospitality businesses, and any organization that wants to purchase Novilla products in bulk at factory-direct pricing. You buy the inventory, set your own retail margin, and sell through your own channels.

### Who Should Apply for the Wholesale Program?

The Wholesale Program is a strong fit if you:

- Own or manage a furniture, mattress, or home goods retail store
- Furnish short-term rentals, hotels, dormitories, or corporate housing
- Work as an interior designer sourcing products for client projects
- Operate an e-commerce store and want to carry Novilla as a brand

## Side-by-Side Comparison

| Feature | Affiliate Program | Wholesale / B2B |
|---|---|---|
| **How you earn** | Commission on referred sales | Margin on products you resell |
| **Inventory required** | No | Yes |
| **Commission / discount** | 6%–10% commission | Up to 30% off MSRP |
| **Cookie / referral window** | 180 days | N/A |
| **Customer discount code** | 5% off for your audience | N/A |
| **Payout method** | Monthly PayPal via Impact/Refersion | Direct order invoicing |
| **Free samples** | Yes | N/A |
| **Best for** | Content creators, influencers | Retailers, hospitality, designers |
| **Registration** | Impact platform | Novilla Business Portal |
