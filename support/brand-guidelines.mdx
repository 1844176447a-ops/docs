When you promote Novilla, you become part of how the world discovers and experiences the brand. Getting the brand voice and visual identity right isn't just about compliance — it's what makes your promotions feel authentic and trustworthy to your audience. These guidelines give you everything you need to represent Novilla accurately, consistently, and compellingly.

## Who Novilla Is

Novilla is a **sustainable lifestyle and sleep brand** on a mission to make a cozy, comfortable home accessible to everyone. The brand tagline — **"A cozy home made simple"** — captures everything Novilla stands for: warmth, simplicity, quality, and a belief that a great night's sleep shouldn't cost a fortune.

Novilla products are designed for everyday people who care about both comfort and value. The brand resonates strongly with first-time homeowners, apartment dwellers, families, and anyone who wants to upgrade their sleep without navigating complicated choices or inflated prices.

## Brand Voice and Tone

Novilla's voice is **warm, genuine, and approachable**. When writing or speaking about Novilla products, aim to sound like a knowledgeable friend who's excited to share a great find — not a salesperson reading from a script.

<Accordion title="Warm and Welcoming">
  Novilla is a home brand at its core. Your content should feel like an invitation into a comfortable space. Use language that evokes coziness, relaxation, and ease — words like *restful*, *comfortable*, *simple*, *cozy*, and *quality* align with the brand.

  **Example:** "I've been sleeping on a Novilla mattress for three months and honestly, I don't know how I went so long without it."
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Honest and Grounded">
  Novilla earns trust through transparency. Avoid hype language, superlatives without evidence, or claims you can't back up. If you're writing a review, be genuine — an honest take from a real person is more valuable than polished copy that sounds too good to be true.

  **Avoid:** "The best mattress ever made, better than anything else on the market."
  **Better:** "For the price, I haven't found anything that comes close in terms of comfort and build quality."
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Accessible and Inclusive">
  Novilla believes that comfort is for everyone. Avoid elitist language or framing that positions quality sleep as a luxury only some people deserve. The brand's accessibility and affordability are selling points — lean into them.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Sustainability-Forward">
  Novilla is committed to sustainable practices and responsible sourcing. When mentioning sustainability, keep it factual and grounded. You can reference that Novilla products are **CertiPUR-US® certified** (for foam products), which means they're made without harmful chemicals and meet strict environmental standards.

  Do not make exaggerated environmental claims. Stick to what Novilla actually certifies and communicates on its official website.
</Accordion>

## Visual Identity

Novilla's visual brand is built around a **warm orange color palette** paired with clean, lifestyle-driven photography that shows products in real, inviting home settings. When selecting or creating visuals for your promotions:

- Use official Novilla images and graphics from your affiliate dashboard wherever possible
- If you're creating your own photography or video, warm, natural lighting in home environments fits the brand best
- Avoid cold, clinical, or minimalist aesthetics that clash with the brand's warm character
- Do not apply heavy filters that significantly alter the color of Novilla products

<Warning>
  Never alter, distort, recolor, or recreate the Novilla logo. Always use the official logo files provided in your affiliate dashboard.
</Warning>

## What NOT to Say or Do

<Accordion title="Do not make false or unverified claims">
  Every claim you make about a Novilla product should be verifiable on the official Novilla website. Do not invent specifications, exaggerate certifications, or promise outcomes (like "guaranteed better sleep") that Novilla does not officially claim.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do not disparage competitors">
  Avoid content that directly attacks, mocks, or disparages competing mattress brands. Novilla's brand position is built on its own merits — let the products speak for themselves.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do not imply an exclusive or official partnership">
  Unless you have a formally documented partnership agreement beyond a standard affiliate relationship, do not describe yourself as Novilla's "official partner," "exclusive ambassador," or similar titles that imply a deeper business relationship than exists.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Do not use the brand in misleading contexts">
  Do not use Novilla's brand name or assets in content that is unrelated to sleep, home, or lifestyle — or in any content that could damage the brand's reputation.
</Accordion>
