## When to Use Each Channel

Not sure which contact to use? Here's a quick reference:

<Accordion title="I have a question about my affiliate commissions or tracking">
  Contact **affiliates@novilla.us**. Include your affiliate ID or the email address associated with your account so the team can pull up your details quickly.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I want to request a product sample for a review or unboxing">
  Contact **pr@novilla.us**. Include a brief overview of your platform (blog, YouTube channel, Instagram, TikTok, etc.), your audience size, and the specific product(s) you'd like to review.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I'm interested in buying Novilla products in bulk or for retail">
  Contact **support@sweetnight.com** or call **1-857-305-6091**. The wholesale team handles B2B pricing, volume orders, and international distribution inquiries.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My audience member has a question about their order or return">
  Direct them to the Novilla customer support page at [novillashop.com/pages/contact-us](https://www.novillashop.com/pages/contact-us). As a partner, you are not responsible for handling customer service — Novilla's team takes care of post-purchase support directly.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I want to pitch a co-marketing campaign or brand collaboration">
  Reach out to **pr@novilla.us** for brand collaboration proposals. For affiliate program expansions or performance-based co-marketing, contact **affiliates@novilla.us**.
</Accordion>
