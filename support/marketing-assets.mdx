One of the biggest advantages of the Novilla Partner Program is that you don't have to build your promotional materials from scratch. Novilla provides a library of ready-to-use marketing assets — from banner ads and text links to social media creatives — all available directly inside your affiliate dashboard. This guide walks you through where to find everything and how to use it effectively.

## Where to Find Your Marketing Assets

All creative materials are housed inside your affiliate dashboard. Depending on which platform you joined through, you'll find assets in one of two places:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Refersion Dashboard" icon="r" href="https://novilla.refersion.com/">
    Log in to your Refersion account and navigate to the **Creatives** or **Assets** section to browse available banners, text links, and promotional codes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Impact Dashboard" icon="i" href="https://app.impact.com/campaign-campaign-info-v2/Novilla.brand">
    Log in to your Impact account and visit the **Assets** tab within the Novilla campaign to access display ads, deep links, and social content.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Types of Marketing Assets Available

<Accordion title="Banner Ads">
  Novilla provides banner ads in multiple standard sizes for use on websites, blogs, and email newsletters. Banners feature Novilla's warm orange color palette and lifestyle photography that resonates with audiences interested in sleep, home comfort, and sustainable living.

  To use a banner, copy the embed code from your dashboard and paste it into your website or email template. Your affiliate tracking ID is automatically embedded in the link.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Text Links">
  Text links are the simplest way to add affiliate tracking to your content. Use them inline within blog posts, buying guides, or review articles. Your dashboard provides pre-built text links for Novilla's homepage, product category pages, and individual product listings.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Discount Codes">
  Exclusive discount codes may be available to share with your audience. Check your dashboard for any active promo codes associated with your affiliate account. When a customer uses your code at checkout, the sale is attributed to you.

  <Tip>
    Discount codes perform especially well in video content, social media posts, and email newsletters where you can speak directly to your audience and explain the offer.
  </Tip>
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Social Media Creatives">
  Pre-designed graphics optimized for Instagram, Facebook, Pinterest, and TikTok are available in your dashboard. These assets follow Novilla's brand guidelines, so you can post them with confidence knowing they represent the brand accurately.

  Don't forget to tag Novilla when you post:
  - Instagram: **@novilla_home**
  - Facebook: **Novilla Home**
  - TikTok: **@novilla_official**
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Deep Links">
  Deep links let you send customers directly to a specific product page rather than the homepage. If you're writing a review of a specific mattress model, use a deep link to that product so there's as little friction as possible between your content and the customer's purchase.
</Accordion>

## Requesting Free Product Samples

If you're a content creator, blogger, or influencer who wants to review Novilla products firsthand, you can request a free sample by contacting the Novilla PR team.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare Your Pitch">
    Before reaching out, put together a brief summary of your platform: your website or social media handle, your audience size and demographics, and the type of content you create (e.g., unboxing video, written review, sleep blog post).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Email the PR Team">
    Send your request to [pr@novilla.us](mailto:pr@novilla.us). Include your platform summary and the specific product(s) you're interested in reviewing. Be clear about your timeline if you have a planned publication date.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Await Confirmation">
    The Novilla PR team will review your request and follow up with product availability and shipping details. Response times may vary based on volume.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create and Share Your Content">
    Once you receive your sample, create honest, authentic content for your audience. Authentic reviews consistently outperform generic promotional posts in both engagement and conversion.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Product samples are provided for review and content creation purposes. You are encouraged to share your genuine experience with the product, including any feedback — honest reviews build the trust that drives long-term affiliate success.
</Note>

## Wholesale and Co-Branded Assets

If you are a wholesale partner or retail reseller, Novilla offers **co-branded marketing assets** that incorporate both the Novilla brand and your business identity. To request co-branded materials, contact [affiliates@novilla.us](mailto:affiliates@novilla.us) with details about your business and intended use.

## Dos and Don'ts for Using Novilla Assets

Following these guidelines protects both you and the Novilla brand, and ensures your promotions remain in good standing within the program.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="✅ Do" icon="circle-check">
    - Use official assets downloaded from your affiliate dashboard
    - Tag Novilla's official social handles when posting
    - Include required affiliate disclosures (e.g., #ad or #affiliate)
    - Use the brand name as "Novilla" with a capital N
    - Represent the products accurately based on official product information
  </Card>
  <Card title="❌ Don't" icon="circle-xmark">
    - Modify logos, brand colors, or official imagery without approval
    - Make price or performance claims not supported by Novilla's website
    - Use Novilla assets in content that promotes competing sleep brands
    - Strip your affiliate ID from links and share "clean" product URLs
    - Imply an official endorsement or partnership beyond your affiliate role
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Warning>
  Using unauthorized or modified brand assets, or making misleading claims about Novilla products, may result in removal from the affiliate program. When in doubt, email [affiliates@novilla.us](mailto:affiliates@novilla.us) before publishing.
</Warning>
