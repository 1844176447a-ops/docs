Novilla backs every mattress with a **100-night sleep trial**, giving customers over three months to decide whether their new mattress is the right fit. This risk-free guarantee is one of the most powerful trust signals you can share as a Novilla partner — it removes the single biggest barrier to buying a mattress online.

## The 100-Night Sleep Trial

Every Novilla mattress purchase comes with a 100-night trial period. Here's how it works:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Purchase and Receive">
    The customer orders their mattress and receives it at home. The 100-night trial begins from the date of delivery.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Try It Out (Minimum 21 Nights)">
    Customers sleep on the mattress and give their body time to adjust. Novilla **recommends at least 21 nights** before deciding — most sleepers need 2–3 weeks to fully adapt to a new sleep surface.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request a Return (If Needed)">
    If the customer isn't satisfied within the 100-night window, they can initiate a free return. Contact Novilla support to begin the process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Free Pickup Arranged">
    Novilla arranges free return pickup within the U.S. The customer does not need to repackage the mattress in its original box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Refund Issued">
    Once the returned product is received by Novilla, a full refund is processed within **5 business days**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  The 100-night sleep trial applies to mattress purchases through **novillashop.com**. Purchases through **novilla.net** are subject to a **30-night** sleep trial. Make sure your audience is purchasing through the correct channel.
</Note>

## Affiliate & Partner Note: Commission Reversals

As a Novilla affiliate, it's important to understand how returns affect your commissions:

<Note>
  **Commission Reversal Policy:** If a customer you referred returns their mattress within the sleep trial period, the associated commission may be reversed. This is standard practice across most affiliate programs.
</Note>

To minimize return rates in your audience:

- **Promote the right mattress** for the right sleeper. Use the product guides to match customers to the best fit (e.g., AiryFlow™ for hot sleepers, Bliss for side sleepers).
- **Set accurate expectations** — mention the 12–72 hour expansion time and the 21-night adjustment period so customers aren't alarmed by initial firmness.
- **Highlight certifications** — CertiPUR-US and OEKO-TEX reassure buyers about safety and quality, reducing buyer's remorse.

## 10-Year Warranty vs. Sleep Trial

The sleep trial and the warranty are two separate customer protections:

<Tabs>
  <Tab title="100-Night Sleep Trial">
    **What it covers:** Personal preference — the customer simply decides the mattress isn't the right fit for them.

    **Duration:** 100 nights from delivery (novillashop.com) or 30 nights (novilla.net)

    **Outcome:** Full refund, free return pickup in the U.S.

    **When to use:** Within the trial window, for any reason.
  </Tab>
  <Tab title="10-Year Limited Warranty">
    **What it covers:** Manufacturing defects and structural issues — sagging beyond normal wear, broken or damaged coils, defective materials.

    **Duration:** 10 years from purchase date

    **Outcome:** Repair or replacement at Novilla's discretion

    **When to use:** After the sleep trial period, if a defect develops over time.
  </Tab>
</Tabs>

## How to File a Warranty Claim

If a customer experiences a defect covered under the 10-year warranty, they should:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Document the Issue">
    Take clear photos or videos of the defect (e.g., sagging, visible damage to materials) along with the mattress label showing the model and serial number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Contact Novilla Support">
    Reach out via the contact page at [novillashop.com/pages/contact-us](https://www.novillashop.com/pages/contact-us) or email the support team with the documentation.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive Resolution">
    Novilla will review the claim and determine whether a repair, replacement, or other resolution is appropriate under the warranty terms.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Direct customers with warranty questions to Novilla's support team directly. As a partner, you don't need to handle warranty claims — just point customers to the right channel.
</Tip>

## HSA/FSA Eligibility

Novilla mattresses are eligible for purchase using **Health Savings Account (HSA)** or **Flexible Spending Account (FSA)** funds. This is a meaningful selling point for customers who have pre-tax healthcare dollars available — effectively making the mattress more affordable.

Mention HSA/FSA eligibility in your content targeting health-conscious buyers or those managing back pain, sleep disorders, or chronic discomfort.
