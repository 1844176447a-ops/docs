Every Novilla order ships free within the United States — no minimum purchase, no hidden fees. As a Novilla partner, this is one of the easiest selling points you can lead with: customers pay exactly what they see at checkout, and their new mattress or bed frame arrives quickly via trusted carriers.

## Shipping Timeline

From the moment an order is placed, customers can expect a smooth, trackable experience:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Order Confirmed">
    The order is processed and prepared for shipment. Customers receive an order confirmation email immediately.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tracking Number Issued">
    A tracking number is sent within **1–2 business days** of order placement. Customers can use this to monitor their shipment in real time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Delivery Arrives">
    Orders are delivered within **3–7 business days** of shipment. Delivery times may vary slightly based on location and carrier availability.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Business days are Monday–Friday, excluding U.S. federal holidays. Orders placed over the weekend are processed on the next business day.
</Note>

## Mattress Setup Instructions

Novilla mattresses are shipped compressed in a box for easy delivery and handling. Once the order arrives, share these setup steps with your audience so they know exactly what to expect:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Unbox on Your Bed Frame">
    Carry the box to the bedroom and place it on your bed frame or flat surface before opening. The mattress is heavy — position it where you want it first.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Remove the Packaging">
    Carefully cut away the outer box and plastic wrap. Use scissors cautiously to avoid cutting the mattress fabric.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Unroll the Mattress">
    Unroll or unfold the mattress onto the bed frame. It will begin to expand immediately upon release from compression.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Allow Time to Fully Expand">
    The mattress needs **12–72 hours** to fully expand to its intended size and firmness. This is completely normal for all foam and hybrid models.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sleep on It">
    The mattress is safe to sleep on within a few hours, even before it has fully expanded. Full comfort and support are achieved after the expansion period.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Remind your audience that a slight off-gassing smell upon unboxing is normal and will dissipate within 24–48 hours. All Novilla mattresses are **CertiPUR-US certified**, meaning they are free from harmful chemicals.
</Tip>
