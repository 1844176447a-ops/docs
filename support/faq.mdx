## General Program Questions

<Accordion title="How do I get started with the Novilla Partner Program?">
  Getting started is free and straightforward:

  1. **Apply** — Sign up through [Refersion](https://novilla.refersion.com/) or [Impact](https://app.impact.com/campaign-campaign-info-v2/Novilla.brand), depending on which platform you were invited to or prefer.
  2. **Get approved** — Once your application is reviewed and approved, you'll receive access to your affiliate dashboard.
  3. **Get your links** — Grab your unique affiliate tracking links and any available creative assets from your dashboard.
  4. **Start promoting** — Share your links through your website, blog, social media, email newsletter, or wherever your audience is most engaged.
  5. **Earn commissions** — Earn 6%–10% on every qualifying sale referred through your links, with a 180-day cookie window.

  Have questions during setup? Reach out to [affiliates@novilla.us](mailto:affiliates@novilla.us).
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Why should I promote Novilla?">
  Novilla is a growing, well-loved sleep and home brand with a track record of high customer satisfaction. Here's why affiliates choose to promote Novilla:

  - **Competitive commissions** — 6% to 10% per sale on products with strong average order values.
  - **180-day cookie** — One of the longest cookie windows in the industry, giving you credit for sales that happen weeks after the first click.
  - **Quality products** — CertiPUR-US® certified foam, hybrid and memory foam options, and a 10-year warranty make Novilla easy to recommend with confidence.
  - **Real support** — Marketing assets, product samples, and a responsive affiliate team at your service.
  - **Brand people trust** — Novilla's generous sleep trials (100 nights on novillashop.com) reduce purchase hesitation and improve conversion rates.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What products can I promote?">

  - **Hybrid mattresses** — Combining innerspring support with foam comfort layers.
  - **Mattress accessories** — Pillows, mattress toppers, bed frames, and bedding.

<Accordion title="Can I promote Novilla on social media?">
  Absolutely. Social media is one of the most effective channels for Novilla affiliates. You can promote on any platform including Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook, Pinterest, and your own blog or website.

  When posting on social media:
  - Tag Novilla's official handles: **@novilla_home** on Instagram, **@novilla_official** on TikTok.
  - Always include an affiliate disclosure in your posts (e.g., "#ad", "#affiliate", or "This post contains affiliate links").
  - Use official creative assets from your dashboard or create original content featuring products you've received as samples.

  <Note>
    Disclosure requirements vary by platform and country. Make sure your disclosures comply with FTC guidelines (in the U.S.) and any relevant local regulations.
  </Note>
</Accordion>

</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What promotional methods are not allowed?">
  To protect the integrity of the program and the Novilla brand, the following promotional methods are prohibited:

  - **Paid search ads on branded keywords** — You may not bid on "Novilla," "Novilla mattress," or similar trademarked terms in paid search campaigns unless explicitly approved in writing.
  - **Coupon and deal sites** — Posting affiliate links on unauthorized coupon aggregator sites is not permitted unless you've been approved as a deal/coupon partner.
  - **Misleading claims** — Any content that contains false product claims, fabricated reviews, or misleading pricing information.
  - **Spam** — Unsolicited email campaigns or bulk message promotion of your affiliate links.
  - **Cookie stuffing or forced clicks** — Any technical manipulation of tracking cookies or clicks.
  - **Content that harms the brand** — Promotions placed alongside content that is offensive, discriminatory, or otherwise damaging to the Novilla brand image.

  If you're unsure whether your intended promotional method is permitted, contact [affiliates@novilla.us](mailto:affiliates@novilla.us) before proceeding.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What are Novilla's shipping and return policies I should know about?">
  Having accurate product information makes you a more trustworthy affiliate. Here's what you should know:

  **Shipping:**
  - Free shipping on all U.S. orders.
  - Tracking number provided within 1–2 business days of purchase.
  - Delivery within 3–7 business days via FedEx or UPS.

  **Returns:**
  - **100-night sleep trial** on [novillashop.com](https://www.novillashop.com).
  - **30-night sleep trial** on [novilla.net](https://www.novilla.net).
  - Free returns within the trial period for U.S. customers.
  - Refund issued within 5 business days of Novilla receiving the returned product.

  **Warranty:**
  - All Novilla mattresses (both memory foam and hybrid) come with a **10-year limited warranty** covering structural and manufacturing defects.

  Always refer customers to the official Novilla website for the most up-to-date policy details.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What happens to my commission if I refer a customer who later returns the product?">
  If a customer returns a product within the applicable return window, your commission for that order will be **reversed**. The commission will be removed from your pending or approved balance in your affiliate dashboard.

  This is a standard practice across affiliate programs and is why Novilla includes a 45-day validation period before commissions are paid out. Focusing on authentic, targeted promotions that attract customers genuinely interested in Novilla products is the best way to keep your return rate low.
</Accordion>
