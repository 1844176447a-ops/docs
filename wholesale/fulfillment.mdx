## International Fulfillment

Novilla exports to over 80 countries and manages international logistics through its established freight network. Here's what to expect for international wholesale orders:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Shipping Cost Calculation">
    International shipping costs are calculated at checkout based on your delivery country, order weight, and shipping method. For large international orders, contact the B2B team before placing your order to receive a freight quote.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export Documentation">
    Novilla handles standard export documentation. For orders requiring specific customs declarations, certificates of origin, or compliance paperwork, notify the B2B team at the time of ordering.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Customs & Duties">
    Import duties, taxes, and customs clearance fees are the responsibility of the receiving partner and are determined by the destination country's regulations. Novilla's B2B team can provide product documentation to support the customs process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tracking International Shipments">
    International orders include tracking through the primary carrier. Depending on the destination country's postal and logistics network, tracking granularity may vary once the shipment clears customs.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
  Novilla's international export experience spans 80+ countries, and the B2B team is familiar with common customs requirements across major markets. If you're shipping to a new or complex destination, reach out before ordering.
</Info>

## Tracking Your Shipment

Once your order ships, tracking information is added to your order record in the B2B portal at [business.sweetnight.com](https://business.sweetnight.com).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log In to Your Account">
    Go to business.sweetnight.com and log in to your partner account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Order History">
    Open your order history and locate the relevant order. Orders in transit will show a "Shipped" status with a tracking number.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Use the Tracking Number">
    Click the tracking number or copy it to the carrier's tracking portal to monitor real-time delivery progress.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Packaging Standards

Novilla's in-house packaging process is designed for wholesale and freight conditions. Mattresses are roll-packed in reinforced poly-film and boxed for protection against moisture and handling impacts. Packaging is designed to:

- Withstand stacking and transit pressure in freight environments
- Maintain mattress shape and integrity through the compression and expansion cycle
- Present cleanly at the point of delivery for retail or hospitality environments

<Tip>
  Instruct receiving staff to inspect packaging on arrival and note any visible damage on the carrier delivery receipt before signing. This is important for any damage claims, even given the extremely low damage rate.
</Tip>

## If Something Goes Wrong

Despite the high reliability of Novilla's fulfillment operation, issues can occasionally arise. Here's how to handle the most common situations:

<Accordion title="My order hasn't arrived by the estimated delivery date.">
  First, check your tracking information in the B2B portal for any carrier updates or delays. If the tracking shows no movement for more than 2 business days past the expected delivery date, contact the B2B team at support@sweetnight.com with your order number. The team will investigate with the carrier and provide an updated timeline or arrange a resolution.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My order arrived with packaging damage.">
  Document the damage immediately — photograph the packaging and the product before moving anything. Note the damage on the carrier's delivery receipt if the driver is present. Then contact the B2B team with your order number and photos within 48 hours of delivery. Novilla will work with you to replace damaged units or issue a credit.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="I received the wrong products or quantities.">
  Compare your delivery against the packing slip included with the shipment and your order confirmation email. If there is a discrepancy, contact the B2B team at support@sweetnight.com with your order number and a description of what was received versus what was expected. Errors will be corrected promptly.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="My international shipment is held in customs.">
  Contact the B2B team and provide your tracking number and any customs reference numbers. The team can supply supporting documentation — commercial invoices, product certifications, or country-of-origin certificates — to help facilitate clearance.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I start a warranty claim on a wholesale order?">
  All Novilla mattresses carry a 10-year warranty. For wholesale accounts, warranty claims are handled through the B2B team. Contact support@sweetnight.com with your order number, the affected unit's details, and a description of the issue. The team will guide you through the claim process.
</Accordion>
