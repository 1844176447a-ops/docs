Novilla's wholesale and dealer program gives businesses direct access to premium sleep products at factory-direct prices, backed by a supply chain that has served partners in over 80 countries. Whether you're a furniture retailer stocking showroom floors, a hotel group outfitting guest rooms, or a bulk buyer sourcing mattresses at scale, the program is designed to make procurement simple, reliable, and cost-effective. All wholesale activity is managed through Novilla's dedicated B2B platform at [business.sweetnight.com](https://business.sweetnight.com).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Register as a Partner">
    Submit your business details at [business.sweetnight.com/pages/b2b-registration](https://business.sweetnight.com/pages/b2b-registration) to unlock wholesale pricing and catalog access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Explore Pricing Tiers">
    Review volume discount tiers and understand how factory-direct pricing applies to your order size. See the [Partner Pricing](/wholesale/partner-pricing) page for a full breakdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place Your First Order">
    Log in to [business.sweetnight.com](https://business.sweetnight.com), browse the catalog, and place your order. Bulk order support is available if you need assistance. See the [Ordering guide](/wholesale/ordering) for details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track Fulfillment">
    Monitor your order status and shipment tracking directly through the B2B portal. Review the [Fulfillment](/wholesale/fulfillment) page for timelines and logistics details.
  </Step>
</Steps>
