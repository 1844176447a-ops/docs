Novilla's wholesale pricing model is built around one principle: because everything is manufactured in-house, savings are passed directly to you as a registered partner. There are no middlemen and no distributor markups. Once you complete the B2B registration, you gain access to a pricing structure that scales with your order volume — starting at 10% off for small multi-unit purchases and reaching up to 30% off when you order at scale. All pricing is managed through [business.sweetnight.com](https://business.sweetnight.com).

## Why Factory-Direct Pricing Matters

Most mattress wholesalers source from third-party factories and layer their own margins on top before passing pricing to resellers. Novilla manufactures 100% of its products in-house, which eliminates that intermediate step entirely. The result is pricing that is consistently **20% lower than comparable factory prices** on the open market — before any volume discount is applied.

<Info>
  Factory-direct pricing is automatically applied to your account once your B2B registration is approved. You do not need to negotiate pricing or request special rates for standard tiers.
</Info>

## Volume Discount Tiers

Your discount level is determined by the number of mattress units in a single order. The tiers below apply to mattress orders specifically; contact the B2B team for bulk pricing on bed frames, bedding, and pillows.

| Order Quantity | Discount Off List Price | Pricing Access |
|---|---|---|
| 1 unit | Standard retail price | Public |
| 2–11 units | **10% off** | Registered partners |
| 12–19 units | **Up to 20% off** | Registered partners |
| 20+ units | **Up to 30% off** | Registered partners |

<Tip>
  If you're planning a large initial order — for example, outfitting a hotel property or stocking a new showroom — placing your first order at the 20+ unit threshold gives you the maximum discount from day one. Contact the B2B team before placing a large order to confirm pricing and discuss any additional incentives.
</Tip>

## How to Unlock Wholesale Pricing

Wholesale pricing is not available to the general public. To access partner rates, you must complete the B2B registration process:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Submit Your Registration">
    Go to [business.sweetnight.com/pages/b2b-registration](https://business.sweetnight.com/pages/b2b-registration) and fill out the registration form with your business name, contact information, and intended use (retail, hospitality, reseller, etc.).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Await Account Approval">
    The Novilla B2B team reviews registrations and approves accounts. You'll receive a confirmation email once your account has been activated with wholesale pricing applied.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log In and View Partner Pricing">
    Once approved, log in to [business.sweetnight.com](https://business.sweetnight.com). Partner pricing will be reflected automatically in the catalog and at checkout — no coupon codes required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Place Your Order">
    Add the products and quantities you need to your cart. Your applicable volume discount tier will be calculated at checkout based on total unit count.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<Accordion title="Do I need a minimum order quantity to register?">
  No. There is no minimum order quantity required to register as a wholesale partner. However, the volume discount tiers begin at 2 units, so orders of a single unit are priced at standard retail rates even for registered partners.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Are discounts applied automatically at checkout?">
  Yes. Once your account is approved and you're logged in to business.sweetnight.com, your partner pricing is applied automatically based on the quantity in your cart. You don't need to enter a discount code.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I mix product types to reach a higher discount tier?">
  Volume discount tiers for mattresses are based on mattress unit count. For bundled orders across multiple product categories, contact the B2B team — they can discuss consolidated pricing based on total order value.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How does the 100-night trial work for wholesale partners?">
  The 100-night risk-free trial is available to qualifying partner programs. Contact support@sweetnight.com to discuss how the trial policy applies to your specific business arrangement.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Who do I contact for a custom quote on a large order?">
  Reach the B2B team directly at **support@sweetnight.com** or call **1-857-305-6091** (Mon–Fri 9am–6pm, Sat–Sun 10am–7pm) to discuss pricing for orders that exceed standard tier thresholds or include mixed product types.
</Accordion>
