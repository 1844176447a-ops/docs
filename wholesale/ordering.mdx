All wholesale orders are placed and managed through [business.sweetnight.com](https://business.sweetnight.com), Novilla's dedicated B2B platform. The portal gives you access to the full product catalog with your partner pricing already applied, a streamlined checkout flow designed for bulk quantities, and order management tools to track purchases after they're placed. Before you can place your first order, you'll need a registered and approved partner account — if you haven't done that yet, start at [business.sweetnight.com/pages/b2b-registration](https://business.sweetnight.com/pages/b2b-registration).

## Placing Your First Wholesale Order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log In to Your Partner Account">
    Go to [business.sweetnight.com](https://business.sweetnight.com) and log in with the credentials you set during registration. Your wholesale pricing tiers are tied to your account and applied automatically once you're signed in.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse the Product Catalog">
    Navigate to the catalog to explore available mattresses, bed frames, bedding, and pillows. Product pages include size options, specifications, certifications, and your partner price for each SKU.

    <Tip>
      Use the catalog filters to sort by product type, size, or price range — especially useful when you're sourcing across multiple categories for a hospitality or staging project.
    </Tip>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Products and Quantities">
    On each product page, choose your size (Twin, Twin XL, Full, Queen, King, California King for mattresses) and enter your desired quantity. Add items to your cart. Your applicable volume discount tier will be reflected in the cart as quantities increase.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review Your Cart">
    Before proceeding to checkout, review your cart for accuracy — product names, sizes, quantities, and pricing. This is also where you'll see your volume discount applied as a line item discount or adjusted unit price.

    <Note>
      If you're placing a large order and want to confirm pricing before finalizing, you can contact the B2B team at support@sweetnight.com or call 1-857-305-6091 to review your cart with a team member.
    </Note>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Shipping Details">
    Provide your delivery address. For U.S. orders, shipping is free on all wholesale orders. For international orders, the platform will calculate shipping based on destination. See the [Fulfillment](/wholesale/fulfillment) page for expected timelines and international shipping details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete Checkout and Receive Confirmation">
    Submit your payment and complete the order. You'll receive an order confirmation email with your order number, itemized summary, and estimated shipping timeline. Save this confirmation for your records.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>

## Order Modifications and Cancellations

<Accordion title="Can I modify an order after it's been placed?">
  Order modifications depend on fulfillment status. If your order has not yet entered the shipping process, the B2B team may be able to adjust quantities or items. Contact support@sweetnight.com as soon as possible with your order number if you need to make a change.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="Can I cancel a wholesale order?">
  Cancellation is possible before an order is dispatched. Once a shipment is in transit, cancellation is not available, but you may be eligible for a return or exchange depending on the product and circumstances. Contact the B2B team to discuss your options.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="What if I ordered the wrong size?">
  If a size error is identified before shipment, contact the B2B team immediately to request a correction. If the order has already shipped, reach out to discuss return or exchange options — the 10-year warranty and quality guarantee apply to all wholesale mattress orders.
</Accordion>

<Accordion title="How do I place a repeat or reorder?">
  You can reorder directly from your order history in the B2B portal. Locate a previous order and use it as a reference to quickly add the same products and quantities to a new cart. Pricing will reflect your current volume discount tier.
</Accordion>
